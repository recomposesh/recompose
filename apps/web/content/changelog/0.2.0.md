recompose 0.2.0 is the first build that leaves the workshop. it exists to prove the whole road from a tag to an installer on every desktop, so later releases can spend their attention on the app instead of the pipeline.

## Features

- the desktop shell opens with liquid glass window chrome on macOS. (#14)
- design tokens paint every surface, in light and in dark. (#20)
- installers build for macOS, Windows, and Linux from one tagged release, with a software bill of materials and provenance attached to every artifact. (#72)
