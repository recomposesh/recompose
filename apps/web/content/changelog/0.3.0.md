recompose 0.3.0 is the release where the gateway becomes real. the app now runs a local gateway, serves it with its own engine, and treats provider subscriptions as accounts it manages for you.

## Features

- the first gateway arrives, with the engine that serves it. (#105)
- provider subscriptions become managed accounts: sign in once, and every gateway can route through that account. (#116)
- the settings screen lands. (#94)
- the sidebar gains its own control, its own edge, and a way out. (#115)
- the app ships its icon and presents itself as Recompose. (#101)

## Fixes

- settings written by a newer build read as newer, not as damage, so a downgrade no longer wipes them. (#98)
